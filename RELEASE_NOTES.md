## New in 1.9.1

Screen sharing now looks like Zoom for your students:

- **The shared screen is about twice as big**: it fills the class window, with the videos beside it
  (or hidden), and "Mr. X is sharing · View Options" at the top.
- **View Options**: Fit to window, 100% (original size), 150%, 200% or 300%, full screen, and show or
  hide the videos. Ctrl + mouse wheel, or a pinch on a touchpad or phone, zooms in; drag to move around.
- **Double-click the shared screen for full screen**: the share fills the whole screen and the
  buttons hide until you move the mouse.
- **Sharper text**: students on laptops with 125% or 150% display scaling now get the full-quality
  picture.
- The class window opens maximized, like Zoom's.

Students in the browser get all of this already (it comes from the website); the app update adds
the bigger class window.

## الجديد في 1.9.1

أصبحت مشاركة الشاشة مثل Zoom عند طلابك:

- **الشاشة المشتركة أكبر بنحو الضعف**: تملأ نافذة الحصة، والفيديوهات بجانبها (أو مخفية)، وفي الأعلى «Mr. X يشارك الشاشة · خيارات العرض».
- **خيارات العرض**: ملاءمة للنافذة، 100% (الحجم الأصلي)، 150%، 200% أو 300%، وملء الشاشة، وإظهار الفيديوهات أو إخفاؤها. ويمكن التكبير بـ Ctrl مع عجلة الفأرة أو بإصبعين، والسحب للتنقل.
- **انقر مرتين على الشاشة المشتركة لملء الشاشة**: تملأ المشاركة الشاشة كلها وتختفي الأزرار حتى تحرك الفأرة.
- **نص أوضح**: الطلاب على أجهزة بتكبير عرض 125% أو 150% يحصلون الآن على الصورة بالجودة الكاملة.
- تفتح نافذة الحصة بالحجم الكامل مثل Zoom.

## New in 1.9.0

LiveClass now works by itself, like the Zoom app, for teachers **and students**:

- **Open LiveClass and sign in** with your email, username or phone and your password. Signed up
  with Google? Use **Sign in with your browser**. You no longer need to go to the website.
- **Home**, like Zoom's: **New Meeting** (teachers) and **Join** tiles, the time, and **your classes**
  with the live ones first. Start, return to, or join a class in one click.
- **Join** with the link your teacher sent (an invite link works without signing in).
- After the class you are back at home; LiveClass no longer closes.
- Start or Join on the website still opens the class here, and now signs LiveClass in too.
- In Arabic when your computer is in Arabic.

Signing in with a password counts as one of your account's devices, like a new browser. Signing in
through the website (its Start button, or Sign in with your browser) does not.

## New in 1.8.0

LiveClass now works like the Zoom app, for every platform (SATAREA, Mr. Shahin, AIS and IGCSE):

- Press **Start LiveClass** on your website: your class opens in LiveClass, **already signed in**.
- No platform to choose, no sign-in screen and no website pages inside the app.
- When the class ends, LiveClass closes.
- Opened by itself, LiveClass only tells you to start your class from your website.
- LiveClass tells you when a new version is out (and **LiveClass → Check for updates…**).

## The first time you install

The app is not signed with a certificate yet, so your computer asks before it opens it:

- **Windows**: "Windows protected your PC" → click **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- **Mac**: open the .dmg and drag LiveClass into Applications. Open it once; when the Mac says it
  can't check it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click
  **Open Anyway**. The first screen share asks for **Screen Recording** permission there too.
